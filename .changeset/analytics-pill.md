The "Cookies" button is now as designed: a round button with a cookie icon, fixed in a bottom corner of the screen, on the banner's surface with an edge and a soft shadow. A theme link marked `data-cookie-settings` no longer hides it for good: the button only steps aside while that link is on screen, and comes back when the link scrolls away. Clicking the icon or the word now works like clicking the button.
