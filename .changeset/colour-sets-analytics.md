Colours for the cookie banner are now a choice, not hex codes. Under Cookie banner > Colours, pick your theme's colours (recommended, as before), one of five ready-made sets (Warm cream with brown buttons, Clean white with blue buttons, Soft grey with green buttons, Pale pink with plum buttons, Dark with gold buttons), or My own colours, which shows the colour boxes as before. Each set has a matching dark version for visitors whose device is in dark mode, every one is easy to read, and Popups offers the same sets, so the banner and your popups can match. Dark mode boxes are now under My own colours, without a separate switch: leave them empty to use the same colours.

Nothing to do on your side: colours you set before show as My own colours and look exactly the same. Choosing a set keeps the colours you typed, so going back to an earlier version, or back to My own colours, shows them again.
