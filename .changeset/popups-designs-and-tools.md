"@moorage/plugin-popups": minor
---

Needs EmDash 1.5.0 or later.

- A second design, "Picture on top", for popups, slide-ins and boxes in a page: the whole picture, never cropped, on a coloured panel (the button colour unless you choose another), with centred words, a full-width button and rounder corners. Existing popups keep the Plain design.
- New optional words: a small label above the heading, a small note under the button, and "Highlight these words" to show part of the heading in the button colour, or in a "Highlight colour" of its own (Admins). The highlighted words must be in the heading and easy to read on your colours.
- "Thanks, I'm not interested" joins the fixed words to say no.
- Popups now use your page's own fonts: the page's text font, and its main heading's font for the heading. This changes the look of existing popups on sites whose theme sets its own fonts.
- A picture can be chosen by an AI assistant's key as well as on the admin page.
- Tools for an AI assistant (MCP): list, get, create, update, turn on and turn off popups, with the admin page's checks and role rules. They are off until an Admin switches them on in EmDash's plugin settings, and no tool deletes a popup.
