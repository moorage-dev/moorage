First release of Backup & Restore: put the whole site back to any moment in the last 30 days from the admin (go back to just before a change, a saved moment, or a time), review what will be undone and copy the text of edits that will be lost, deal with form enquiries first, tick and confirm, then undo with one button. Works on sites that run on Cloudflare, with a key from the owner's own Cloudflare account.
