Clearer kinds, and a helpful "Popups box" list in the editor. Only "Box in a page" is called a box now: a popup "opens by itself over the page, in the middle of the screen", a slide-in "slides in at a bottom corner", and a box in a page "sits inside a page or post, where you place it while editing". When there is no box yet, the editor's "Which box" list says how to make one, and names a popup or slide-in you made instead, since those open by themselves and are not placed in a post. A box that is turned off still shows, marked "(off)".
