The cookie banner now speaks your site's language. On pages marked as German, French, Spanish, Italian, Dutch or Portuguese (the `lang` of your pages), visitors read the banner, the standard heading and text included, in that language; English pages look exactly as before. Under Banner > Button words you can put Accept all, Reject all, Choose, Save my choices and the small Cookies button in your own words; a heading or text you typed yourself is shown as typed in every language.

YouTube and Vimeo videos and Google maps in your pages now wait for consent too. Where the banner can ask, visitors first see a placeholder that says which company shows the video or map, with a button to load just that one and a link to open it on its own site; it appears by itself once they allow Marketing, and straight away for visitors who are not asked. This covers playlists and imported embed codes with a YouTube or Vimeo player too, and pages you exclude from tracking, where they show only for visitors who allowed Marketing. Nothing for editors to do. Sites where nobody is asked see no change. Once a page with one has been shown, the Status tab says which providers your pages use, and an Admin's **Name these in the banner** has the banner name them (for example "Marketing also covers videos and maps from YouTube and Google Maps.", in the site's language) and Choose lists them under Marketing, or, on a site with no Marketing tools, offers a switch of their own, Videos and maps; visitors who answered before are asked again once. **Stop naming** takes a provider back. Sites without such videos or maps keep exactly the banner they had.

Four more tools, one box each: Plausible and Umami Cloud (visitor counts without cookies, so on their own they need no banner), and LinkedIn Insight Tag and TikTok Pixel (they wait for Marketing).

Tools no longer load on a copy of your site on a workers.dev or pages.dev address or on your laptop (localhost), unless that is the Site URL in Settings > General: the banner still shows, but nothing is tracked and no answer is recorded. Every other domain is tracked as before, so a Site URL left on an old address never stops tracking on your live site. If you open the admin on an address where tools do not load, the Status tab says so.

Also new: an Ask everyone again button on the Banner tab; the consent log keeps the country the server saw rather than the one the browser reported; other scripts can read the visitor's answer from `window.cookieConsent` and a `cookie-consent:change` event; and the banner keeps working across page changes on sites that use Astro's `<ClientRouter />`. The README, banner and icon now use the plugin's name, Analytics & Cookies.
