**Use with an AI assistant (MCP).** Two read-only tools for AI assistants connected to your site through EmDash's MCP server: `queue` lists the pages waiting for approval (those waiting for you, and your own), and `status` shows one page's approval as its Approval panel does (whether publishing it needs approval, waiting, approved, sent back or published, by whom and when, the latest history lines). Each person gets only what the Approvals page and the panel show them; neither tool returns email addresses, and neither publishes or changes anything. The tools are off until an Admin switches them on for this plugin in EmDash. When you update from the Registry, EmDash asks you to approve the new tools first.

Needs EmDash 1.5.0 or later.
