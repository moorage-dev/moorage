The choices under "Who sees the cookie banner" now say what each is for: where the law says ask first (the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland, recommended if you only count visits), those places plus India, everyone everywhere (recommended if you use Clarity, Meta Pixel or Google Ads), or no one when another consent tool asks instead. A line under them explains that visitors who are not asked, for example in the US, can always turn the tools off with Cookie settings and that Global Privacy Control is respected. The Status tab suggests asking everyone when Clarity, Meta Pixel or Google Ads is on and only Europe is asked. Nothing changes for visitors.
