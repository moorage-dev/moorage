### Why does it say "could not check" instead of broken?

Some websites, such as LinkedIn and Instagram, turn away every automatic checker. Calling those links broken is the most common complaint about link checkers, so they are listed apart and never counted. Open one to check it yourself.

### Why do links to other sites take a day?

A link is called broken only when the other website says the page is gone on two checks at least a day apart. A site that is down for an hour should not send you an alert.

### Will it slow my site down or annoy other websites?

No. It reads your pages a few at a time in the background, asks other websites for a few pages a minute, never the same website more than once a minute, and says who it is.

### Can it change the links inside my pages for me?

Not yet. For an address on your own site, "Send visitors to another page" fixes the link everywhere at once. For other links, it opens the page with the link named.

### My site is not public yet. Does it work?

Links inside your pages are checked against your own pages straight away. Menus, page errors and the Checklist start once the site has a public address.

### Who gets the emails?

The first Admin who opens Site Health, plus anyone you add under Checklist, Options. Your site needs an email service for EmDash; the Checklist's "Send a test email" tells you whether it has one.
