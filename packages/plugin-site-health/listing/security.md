## Reporting a problem

Email **security@moorage.dev**, not a public issue. Say which version you use, what you found and how to reproduce it. Fixes go into the latest release; update to it to receive them. The source is public on GitHub.

## What it keeps and sends

It keeps the problems it found and your choices (marked as fine, alert addresses) in the site's own database. It requests the site's own pages and the addresses your pages link to, and nothing else; it sends no page content to anyone. Alerts go by the site's own email to the addresses you enter. Adding a redirect happens only when you press Fix.
