## How do I make EmDash ask for approval before publishing?

Install this plugin, open **Approvals > Settings**, tick the content types that need approval and press **Save**. From then on, pressing Publish sends the page for approval.

## Who approves?

Choose "Others need my OK before publishing" and site admins approve everyone else's changes. Choose "I need someone else's OK before publishing" and everyone needs approval, admins too; an editor or another admin approves. Nobody approves their own request.

## Why does the editor say "Failed to publish"?

That title is EmDash's own and a plugin cannot change it. The text under it starts "Sent for approval. Not live yet.": nothing failed, the page is waiting for approval.

## Can the approver publish straight away?

Yes. **Approve and publish** approves the exact version they looked at and puts it live in one step.

## Can I see what was approved later?

Yes. The History tab keeps a text copy of every approved version and who approved it.

## What if the approver spots a typo?

They edit and save the page like anyone else, then approve. The history records that the approver edited it.

## Does it work without email?

Yes. Requests show on the dashboard and on the Approvals page. With email set up, approvers are emailed when someone asks.

## What does approval not cover?

Things EmDash saves straight to the live page: the SEO panel, categories, tags and bylines of a page already live, and content types that keep no drafts. The Settings tab lists them for your site.

## Is it free?

Yes, on any number of sites, with no account and no key. It is open source under the MIT License.
