## Reporting a problem

Email **security@moorage.dev**, not a public issue. Say which version you use, what you found and how to reproduce it. Fixes go into the latest release; update to it to receive them. The source is public on GitHub.

## What it keeps and sends

It keeps requests, decisions with their notes and the text of each page's latest approval in the site's own database. It reads users' names and email addresses only to address approvers. Email goes by the site's own email service; nothing is sent to any outside service.
