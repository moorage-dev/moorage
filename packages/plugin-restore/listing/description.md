Put your whole EmDash site back to any moment in the last 30 days, from the admin, and undo it if you picked the wrong time. For the day an import overwrites 31 product prices, a menu change goes wrong, or someone deletes the wrong things.

- **Go back to just before a change.** A list of recent changes, newest first (what, who, when), with a button on each: "Go back to 11:04". A burst such as an import of 31 products is one row.
- **Or pick a moment:** a saved moment ("Before the menu import"), or a day, hour and minute in your site's time zone.
- **Review first.** What will be undone, the text of edits that will be lost (to copy and make again), and who gets access back and who loses it, by name. Nothing changes until you tick the box and confirm.
- **Enquiries are never lost silently.** Form enquiries that came in since the moment are named, and you download them or say they reached you before going back. Lead Delivery's own log is put back afterwards.
- **Undo every restore** with one button, for as long as the history reaches.
- **Email** whoever looks after the site when it is put back or when Restore stops working.

What it is not: not an off-site backup (Cloudflare keeps the history in the same account as your site; for a copy to keep elsewhere, use Settings > Backups); nothing before it was set up, so set it up the day a site launches; no pictures (files are never deleted, and deleted ones don't come back); up to 30 days (7 on Cloudflare's free plan).

It works on sites that run on Cloudflare (D1), with a key you make in your own Cloudflare account. On other sites the page says so and points to Revisions, the Trash and Settings > Backups.
