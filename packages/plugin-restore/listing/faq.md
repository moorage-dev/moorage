**Is this a backup?** Not an off-site one. Cloudflare keeps 30 days of your site's history (7 on the free plan) in the same account as the site. For a copy to keep somewhere else, download one from Settings > Backups.

**Can I go back to before I installed it?** No. Going back to before setup would also remove Restore and the way to undo, so it only offers moments after setup.

**What about pictures?** Picture files are never deleted by a restore, and deleted files don't come back. Pictures added since the moment leave the media library; the files stay in storage.

**What happens to form enquiries?** Enquiries received since the moment are named before you confirm. Download them from Forms > Submissions or say they reached you; the page will not go on until you do.

**Can I undo a restore?** Yes, with "Undo this restore", which is itself a restore to just before. If the admin is ever unreachable, the success screen holds instructions for whoever set up your site.

**The key can reach other sites.** Cloudflare can't limit this permission to one database, so the page lists every site the key can change. Keep each client in a separate Cloudflare account where you can.

**My site isn't on Cloudflare.** Then going back in time isn't available. Use Revisions, the Trash, Settings > Backups, or your host's own restore.
