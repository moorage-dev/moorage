## How do I add schema markup to EmDash?

Install this plugin. Every page gets structured data (JSON-LD) straight away; say who the site is for on the SEO page to make it accurate.

## Is there a Yoast alternative for EmDash?

Yes, in two parts. EmDash itself does titles, descriptions, sitemaps and canonicals. This plugin adds the schema graph, the list of pages to fix, the Google preview and the redirects import. There is no traffic light and no focus keyphrase.

## How do I import redirects from WordPress?

Export them from Redirection, Yoast SEO Premium or Rank Math, open SEO > Redirects > Import from WordPress, paste the list and press "Check list". Nothing is added until you confirm.

## How do I noindex a collection in EmDash?

Open SEO > Settings > Content types and choose "Hide from Google and other search engines" for that content type.

## Is it free?

Yes, on any number of sites, your own or your clients', with no account and no key. It is open source under the MIT License: you may use, change, copy and share the code, in paid work too, as long as the copyright notice stays with it.

## Is there an SEO score?

No, on purpose. You get a list of things to fix that can reach zero.

## Does it add local business schema with opening hours?

Yes. Choose "A local business customers visit" on the SEO page and fill in the address, phone number and opening hours. Until there is an address the site is described as an organisation, because Google requires one.

## Will my pages get rich results on Google?

The markup makes pages eligible; Google decides what it shows. Admins can open Google's Rich Results Test from the Google preview panel.

## Where do I write the meta description?

In EmDash's own SEO panel on each entry. This plugin lists the pages that have none.
